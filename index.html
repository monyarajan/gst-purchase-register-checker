What it checks

GSTIN format

Validates the GSTIN pattern and checksum when a GSTIN field is mapped.

Duplicates

Flags repeated GSTIN + invoice-number combinations.

Tax structure

Flags CGST/SGST mismatch and entries with both IGST and CGST/SGST.

Tax plausibility

Compares tax amount with common GST rates. Treat this as a warning for review, not a filing decision.
